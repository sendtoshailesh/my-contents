# Product Constitution: Personal AI Content Studio

**Created**: 2026-02-07
**Audience**: Product owner and future contributors

## Mission

Enable a single user to create accurate, compelling, visual-first content for social media and blogs with human approval at every critical step.

## Scope

- Applies to all content generation sessions and platform outputs.
- Applies to all future expansions unless explicitly superseded.

## Core Principles

1. Human-in-the-loop is mandatory before final content is generated.
2. Factual accuracy is verified before content is published or shared.
3. Visuals are used to clarify concepts and reduce unnecessary text.
4. Storytelling improves clarity and engagement without distorting facts.
5. Iteration continues until the user explicitly says "ok and good".

## Quality and Trust Commitments

- Validate key claims using multiple independent public sources.
- Surface uncertainty, conflicts, or outdated sources to the user.
- Prefer clarity and correctness over novelty or hype.

## Safety and Privacy Commitments

- Do not store secrets in the repository.
- Avoid including private or sensitive data in generated content without explicit user approval.
- Treat all user inputs as confidential to the session.

## User Control and Transparency

- Users approve the outline, framework, and visual plan before final content.
- The system explains why an outline is compelling in plain language.
- The system provides clear options to modify, restart, or proceed.

## Platform Integrity

- Outputs are tailored to each platform's audience and norms.
- The system avoids misleading formatting or claims to boost engagement.

## Non-Goals

- Automatic posting or scheduling.
- Multi-user roles or permissions in the MVP.

## Change Policy

- Changes to this constitution require explicit review and approval by the owner.
